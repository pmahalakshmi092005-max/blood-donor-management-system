Why Donate Blood?

Blood cannot be manufactured in a factory. It can only come from generous donors like you.

Save Up to 3 Lives

Every single whole blood donation can be separated into red cells, platelets, and plasma, helping multiple patients in critical care.

Critical Hospital Need

Hospitals require constant blood supplies for accident emergencies, surgical procedures, anemia, and cancer chemotherapy support.

Mini Health Checkup

Donors receive a quick physical health assessment including pulse, blood pressure, and hemoglobin levels before every donation.

Replenishes Quickly

The human body naturally regenerates the donated plasma in 24 to 48 hours, and red blood cells within just a few weeks.
